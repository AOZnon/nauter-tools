IMPORTANT - VTP / extended VLAN prerequisite
All VLAN IDs above are extended-range VLANs (1006-4094).
Before creating them, run:

show vtp status
show vlan brief

On Catalyst 2960 with VTP version 1 or 2, extended-range VLAN creation requires VTP transparent mode.
Do NOT change VTP mode blindly on an existing network. If the switch is VTP v1/v2 server/client, confirm the VTP design first.
VTP version 3 can support extended-range VLANs in the appropriate server/transparent configuration.
